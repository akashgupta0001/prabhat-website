Established in 2015 and based in Bahadurgarh, Haryana, PRABHAT INDUSTRIES is engaged in footwear manufacturing and trading.

The company offers a product portfolio covering men's footwear, women's footwear, kids' footwear, sports shoes, slippers and sandals. Its brands, SUPREMLITE and STON, give customers a central place to explore available footwear models and submit enquiries.

Our business

Our business combines manufacturing and trading activities within the footwear sector. We aim to serve a range of customer requirements by presenting our available products clearly and making product enquiries straightforward.

Who we serve

We welcome enquiries from retailers, wholesalers, distributors, bulk purchasers and individual customers.
